<objective>
Give authorised staff an audited housekeeping lifecycle for rooms (ROOMS-02). After this plan, a housekeeper or receptionist moves a room along the D-04 transition table through `PATCH /cms/rooms/{room}/status`, every accepted change leaves a `room_status_history` row, and `rooms.status` holds housekeeping state only (D-01..D-07).

Purpose: this is the first operational status-plus-history feature. Phase 6 (housekeeping tasks) and Phase 7 (tickets) copy its shape, so the transition table, single writer, row lock and history write must be clean. It also retires the stored in-house value that conflated occupancy with housekeeping (Pitfall 1).
Output: two migrations, the extended enum, a history model, a domain exception, an action, a request, a controller verb, a route, the `rooms.status` permission with presets, five-locale keys, fixed demo seeders, and tests.
</objective>

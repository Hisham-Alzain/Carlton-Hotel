<objective>
Staff keep free-text notes on a reservation (RESV-01; D-11, D-13, D-14). After this plan, `PATCH /cms/reservations/{reservation}/notes` sets, replaces and clears notes, the staff reservation detail shows them, and no guest response ever carries them.

Purpose: the first desk verb, and the thinnest end-to-end slice of the phase (migration → request → action → resource → route). It also adds the staff-only `notes` key to the shared `ReservationResource` that every later plan of this phase returns.
Output: one additive migration, `UpdateReservationNotesRequest`, `UpdateReservationNotesAction`, `ReservationController::updateNotes`, `ReservationService::updateNotes`, the route, the `messages.reservation_notes_updated` key in five locales, `ReservationNotesTest`.
</objective>

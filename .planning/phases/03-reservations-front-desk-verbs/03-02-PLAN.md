<objective>
Give the desk a pick list of rooms it can assign to a reservation (RESV-02; D-04, D-12, D-13), and lay down the two shared rules the check-in verb reuses: one availability predicate/picker (D-04) and the hotel-local business date (D-02). After this plan, `GET /cms/reservations/{reservation}/available-rooms` answers in three queries, and the room board and grids default to the hotel's own "today".

Purpose: one predicate for booking, assign-room, check-in and the pick list removes the drift `CheckAvailabilityAction`'s own comments warn about; one hotel clock keeps the check-in window and the board from disagreeing about "today" (research Pitfall 4).
Output: `CheckAvailabilityAction::{isRoomFree, freeRoomsFor, pickRoomFor}`, `ReservationService::availableRooms`, `ReservationController::availableRooms`, the route, `config/hotel.php`, `App\Support\HotelClock`, `Reservation::isWithinStayWindow`, the `FrontDeskService` default-date alignment, and their tests.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-02-1.** The pick list applies D-04's capacity rule before listing: when other overlapping holds (null-room rows included) already use every active room of the type, the list is empty even if a specific room looks physically free. This is booking's own rule, applied so the list and check-in auto-pick never disagree.
- **FA-02-2.** Every filter applies to the assigned room too. An assigned room that went into maintenance, or that another stay names (data conflict), is omitted, and check-in with no `room_uuid` then fails on it (`room_out_of_order` / `room_already_assigned`), which prompts the desk to pick from the list.
- **FA-02-3.** D-04 says the shared predicate is "used by booking". Booking shares the overlap predicate, but keeps `findFreeRoom` (habitability-blind, lowest number) as its picker. Excluding maintenance at booking time would let availability report a free room that booking then refuses (research Pitfall 2). The available-before-dirty picker is used for check-in only.

<objective>
Narrow `assign-room` to pure assignment (RESV-03 "assign-room becomes pure assignment"; D-03, D-04, D-05) and migrate the four tests that pinned the old "assign-room checks in" behaviour. After this plan, the only way to reach `checked_in` is the check-in verb from 03-03; `assign-room` assigns before arrival or moves a guest during the stay, and says so in its response.

Purpose: removes the second, unguarded path into `checked_in` (no window, no lock, no maintenance check), and keeps "room ready" meaning exactly "your room is ready now".
Output: narrowed `AssignRoomAction`, updated controller comment, `AssignRoomTest`, four rewritten test files.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-06-1.** `GuestEntitlement::currentReservation()` picks the booked reservation with the latest check_in. A checked-in guest who also holds a later confirmed booking therefore now gets 422 `reservation_state` from `POST /folio/approve` (before this phase, that call silently checked out the future booking). Correcting the selection is out of scope here and is carried forward to Phase 4 (Guests & Stay); the atomicity test pins that nothing is half-written.
- **FA-06-2.** `folio_status` matches only reservations that have a folio; a reservation whose folio was never generated matches neither `open` nor `settled`. After this phase every checked-out stay has a folio.
- **FA-06-3.** `?status` values are not checked against the enum (an unknown value returns an empty page), like every other plain-column filter on `BaseFilter`; only `folio_status`, which is custom, is validated.

<objective>
Give the desk append-only internal notes on a guest and open the staff `guests` area (GUEST-03 and the notes-list part of GUEST-01; D-01, D-06, D-07). After this plan, reception and concierge can add a note with `POST /guests/{guest}/notes` and read the newest-first list with `GET /guests/{guest}/notes`, while no guest-facing response and no activity-log row ever carries a note.

Purpose: the notes surface is the smallest end-to-end slice of the staff guest area; it seeds `guests.view` / `guests.edit` (XCUT-01) and creates the `GuestController` / `GuestService` / `guests` route group every later staff plan (02, 07, 08) extends.
Output: `guest_notes` migration, `GuestNote` model + factory, `Guest::notes()`, the two permissions with their presets, `AddGuestNoteRequest`, `AddGuestNoteAction`, `GuestNoteResource`, `GuestService`, `Admin\GuestController`, the `guests` route group, one lang key in five locales, `GuestNotesTest`, updated seeder baselines.
</objective>

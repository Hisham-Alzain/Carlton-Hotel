<objective>
Let guests and staff save guest preferences (GUEST-04; D-08, D-09). After this plan, the guest app saves bed, pillow, floor and a free-text note through `PATCH /auth/guest/preferences`, the desk does the same through `PATCH /guests/{guest}/preferences`, and `GET /auth/guest/me` shows them, while the allergy-adjacent pillow choice and the free text stay out of the activity log.

Purpose: preferences feed the pre-arrival checklist (`preferences_set`, plan 04-03) and the staff profile (plan 04-08).
Output: additive guests migration, `PillowType` and `FloorPreference` enums, `Guest` casts/fillable and the first per-model `getActivitylogOptions()` override, `UpdateGuestPreferencesRequest`, `UpdateGuestPreferencesAction`, `GuestPreferencesResource`, the `preferences` object on `GuestResource`, two routes, one lang key in five locales, `GuestPreferencesTest`.
</objective>

<objective>
Show guests their pre-arrival state on the stay screens (GUEST-05 exposure side, GUEST-02 checklist builder; D-05, D-11, D-12). After this plan, `GET /stays/upcoming|active|status` return the online check-in state, the digital key while it is active, and the derived six-item checklist, all from one shared builder, with no-store caching and no N+1.

Purpose: this is the read half of online check-in and the digital key. Plan 04-04 adds the submission, 04-05 issues keys, 04-06 revokes them, 04-08 reuses the checklist on the staff profile.
Output: the reservations migration with every online-check-in and key column, the `Reservation` model changes (encrypted hidden non-fillable code, hasOne approval, active-key rule, activity-log override), `StayPayload`, `PreArrivalChecklist`, three stay resources, `StayService` eager loads, no-store headers, `StayPayloadTest`, `PreArrivalChecklistTest`.
</objective>

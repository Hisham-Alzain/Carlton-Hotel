<objective>
Let a guest complete online check-in by submitting an arrival time (GUEST-05 submission; D-10). After this plan, `POST /stays/{reservation}/online-check-in` records the hotel-local arrival time for the guest's own confirmed stay up to and including the check-in day, opens a pending check-in approval if none exists (never touching a decided one), and answers with the upcoming-stay payload built in 04-03.

Purpose: the guest-side action behind the "online check-in" screen; the approval it opens is what staff decide in 04-05 to issue the key.
Output: `OnlineCheckInClosedException`, two lang keys in five locales, `SubmitOnlineCheckInRequest`, `SubmitOnlineCheckInAction`, `StayService::onlineCheckIn`, `StayController::onlineCheckIn`, the route, `OnlineCheckInTest`, `SubmitOnlineCheckInActionTest`.
</objective>

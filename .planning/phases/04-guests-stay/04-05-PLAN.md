<objective>
Issue the digital key when staff approve check-in, revoke it when they reject, and tell the guest the key is ready (GUEST-05 issuance; D-11, D-14). After this plan, approving a confirmed or in-house stay mints a random, encrypted, hashed display code that expires at the hotel's check-out time on the check-out date; re-approval keeps an active key, reject revokes it, a later approval mints a new one, and a push says "your key is ready in the app" without ever carrying the code.

Purpose: the key lifecycle's start; 04-06 finishes it (check-out, cancel, expiry sweep, leakage matrix).
Output: `hotel.check_out_time` config and env, `HotelClock::checkOutAt`, `NotificationType::CHECK_IN_APPROVED`, `DigitalKeyRevocationReason`, `CheckInApproved`, the notification lang pair, `IssueDigitalKeyAction`, `RevokeDigitalKeyAction`, the `ApproveCheckInAction` hook, `SendCheckInApprovedNotification`, the expiry recompute on `Reservation`, three test classes.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-4.05-1.** `CheckInApproved` fires only when the approval changes into approved (previous status not approved) and the stay is confirmed or checked in; a repeated approved decision or an approval of a cancelled/checked-out stay sends no "key ready" push, because the push text is only true when a key exists. D-14 says "on approved" without these qualifiers.
- **FA-4.05-2.** "No-op without an active key" (D-11) is read as "no-op without an issued, unrevoked key": an expired but unrevoked key is still revoked, which the expiry sweep (04-06) relies on.
- **FA-4.05-3.** A document upload (`SubmitDocumentsAction`, unchanged) re-opens an approved approval as pending but does not revoke an active key; the checklist then shows the approval as pending while the key stays valid until a reject, check-out, cancellation or expiry. Re-approval keeps the same key.
- **FA-4.05-4.** A reservation approved while `pending` gets no key and gets none when later confirmed (`ConfirmReservationAction` is not an issuance point in D-11); staff re-approve to issue.
- **FA-4.05-5.** Demo seeders create approved approvals directly (no key); a fresh `migrate:fresh --seed` therefore shows no keys until an approval is made through the endpoint.
- **FA-4.05-6.** `IssueDigitalKeyAction` answers 201 when it mints and 200 when it keeps or skips; callers treat both as success.

<objective>
Turn the folio into an append-only ledger foundation (FOLIO-02 groundwork; D-03, D-04, D-06, D-07). After this plan a guest refreshing `GET /api/folio` sees the same item uuids every time, rows that a later plan posts (manual charges, credits) survive every refresh, and every total and balance is computed exactly with bcmath instead of floats.

Purpose: every later plan (read, post, credit, payment, dispute) stands on three things built here: the additive `folio_items` ledger columns, the one shared balance helper (`Folio::ledgerPayments()/paidUsd()/balanceDueUsd()` over `App\Support\FolioLedger`), and a `GenerateFolioAction` that reconciles under its own folio row lock instead of deleting and rebuilding.
Output: migration `2026_09_26_130000_add_ledger_columns_to_folio_items_table`, enum `FolioItemSource`, `FolioLedger`, the `Folio`/`FolioItem` model additions, factory states `manual()`/`credit()`, the reconcile rewrite, the receipt refactor, the lock-recording test helper, `FolioLedgerTest`, `GenerateFolioReconcileTest` and the strengthened `ExpressCheckoutTest`.
</objective>

<objective>
Let the desk read a reservation's folio (FOLIO-01; D-01, D-02, D-03). After this plan, reception opens `GET /cms/reservations/{reservation}/folio` and sees every line (with who posted it and what it reverses), every payment of the folio and of the reservation, and a signed balance; a reservation without a folio answers 404 `folio_missing` so the dashboard knows to call the existing generate route.

Purpose: the read is the screen every later write returns to; its one `FolioResource` shape (plus one display loader in `FolioService`) is reused by the guest folio, generate, settle, approve and every Phase 5 write.
Output: `FolioMissingException`, `errors.folio_missing` in five locales, `FolioService::adminShow` and its display loader, the extended `FolioResource`/`FolioItemResource`, `Admin\FolioController::showForReservation`, the route, `FolioReadTest`.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-5.03-1 (kind narrowed until 05-04).** This plan accepts `kind: charge` only; plan 05-04 widens the rule to `charge|credit` together with the credit floors, so no credit can be posted without its bounds.
- **FA-5.03-2 (poster not part of an item replay).** D-08 lists the item replay fields (kind, description, quantity, unit_price_usd, reason, reverses_item_uuid) without the poster, so an identical replay by another `folios.post` holder is a 200 replay. Payments compare `recorded_by` (plan 05-05).
- **FA-5.03-3 (attribute names).** D-17 mentions "attribute names"; the codebase has no `custom.attributes` block and no `attributes()` override, so validation messages keep raw field names as in every earlier phase. No attribute block is added.
- **FA-5.03-4 (numeric max wording).** `custom.validation.max`/`min` say "characters" for every field (pre-existing, e.g. the settle request's `min:0.01`); numeric bounds on money inherit that wording. Not changed here (out of scope), carried forward.

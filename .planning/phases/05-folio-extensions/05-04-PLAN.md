| T-05-05 | Information Disclosure / Tampering (cross-folio reference) | `reverses_item_uuid` | medium | mitigate | `Rule::exists` scoped to the route folio id, re-read inside the action on the locked folio; foreign uuid answers 422 without revealing the other folio |

<objective>
Close prepaid folios without a fake payment, and stop money being taken at the reservation level once the folio is closed (FOLIO-04 close slice; D-14, D-15). After this plan a folio already covered by a reservation deposit closes through the existing settle route with no payment row and passes the check-out gate; settling twice answers `folio_settled` everywhere; and the legacy reservation settle refuses once the folio is settled.

Purpose: completes ROADMAP success criterion 3 ("either way the folio then passes the Phase 3 check-out gate") and makes `folio_settled` the one code for "already settled".
Output: `SettleFolioAction` close path, nullable `SettleFolioRequest.amount_usd`, the `required_with` mapping, `FolioService::adminSettle` without float, the controller message choice, the `PaymentService` guard, two lang keys in five locales, updated `FolioTest`/`PaymentTest`, close-path tests in `FolioPaymentTest`.
</objective>

This plan completes "take payments" for prepaid stays.

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-5.06-1 (no amount while money is due).** D-14 does not say what `{}` means on a folio that still owes money. Chosen: 422 `validation_failed` with `errors.amount_usd` (no new error code), raised under the lock so a charge posted between validation and settle is seen.
- **FA-5.06-2 (sent amount ignored at zero balance).** D-14 settles "without a Payment row when balanceDueUsd() <= 0.00"; an amount sent in that case is not recorded (taking money that is not owed would be an overpayment, which D-13 refuses on the payments route). The response message says no payment was recorded.
- **FA-5.06-3 (method optional without an amount).** Only `amount_usd` is named nullable in D-14; `method` becomes `required_with:amount_usd` so a close request can be `{}`. A request with an amount and no method is still 422, as before.
- **FA-5.06-4 (reservation lock in the legacy guard).** The guard locks the reservation before the folio to keep the project-wide lock order; the legacy route took no lock before.

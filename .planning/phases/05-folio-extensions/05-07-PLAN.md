<objective>
Let a guest dispute a line item on their own bill (FOLIO-03 guest slice; D-02, D-06, D-09, D-10, D-15). After this plan a checked-in guest disputes an item with `PATCH /folio/items/{item}/dispute`, sees the dispute status on every item of `GET /folio`, cannot see or touch another guest's items (404), and a disputed charge is frozen through every refresh. Disputes never move money.

Purpose: introduces the dispute history table and the one-open-dispute rule that the staff raise/resolve plan (05-08) and the open-dispute flags plan (05-09) build on.
Output: `folio_item_disputes` migration, `FolioDisputeStatus`, `FolioItemDispute` + factory, `FolioItem::disputes()/latestDispute()` and the dispute freeze, `FolioItemDisputeOpenException`, `RaiseFolioDisputeAction`, `GuestFolioDisputeRequest`, `FolioService::guestDispute`, the item `dispute` field, `Api\FolioController::dispute`, the guest route, two lang keys in five locales, `FolioDisputeTest` (guest matrix), a freeze case in `GenerateFolioReconcileTest`.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-5.07-1 (own folio = any reservation of the guest).** D-10 says "item not on the guest's own folio → 404"; ownership is the item's folio reservation `guest_id`, so a checked-in guest may also dispute items on another of their own stays' folios (past or future). The `is_checked_in` gate still applies to the route.
- **FA-5.07-2 (raised_by when the raiser was deleted).** `raised_by` is `staff` when `user_id` is set, `guest` when `guest_id` is set, and null when the raiser account was deleted (both FKs are nullOnDelete).
- **FA-5.07-3 (resolution note is guest-visible).** D-02 fixes one item shape; the guest sees `resolution_note`, so staff write it for the guest.
- **Prohibition canon-referral.** "A guest must not learn whether another guest's item exists" is an access-control (OWASP IDOR/enumeration) rule, so per the spec-less probe's canon-referral step it is not minted as a prohibition; it is enforced by T-05-05 and the 404 truths above.
- **FA-5.07-4 (any dispute freezes).** A row with any dispute (open, resolved or rejected) is frozen, per D-06 "referenced by … any dispute".

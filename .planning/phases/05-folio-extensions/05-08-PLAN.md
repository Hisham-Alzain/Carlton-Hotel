<objective>
Let the desk raise, resolve and reject line-item disputes (FOLIO-03 staff slice; D-09, D-11, D-15; XCUT-01). After this plan reception raises a dispute on a guest's behalf, closes it as resolved or rejected with a note the guest can read, and settles a refund-worthy dispute by posting a credit; resolving never moves money by itself.

Purpose: completes the dispute workflow and seeds `folios.dispute`.
Output: `folios.dispute` on reception, `FolioDisputeStateException`, `StaffFolioDisputeRequest`, `ResolveFolioDisputeAction`, `FolioService::adminDispute`, `Admin\FolioController::dispute`, the scoped-binding route, three lang keys in five locales, the staff matrix in `FolioDisputeTest`, updated seeder baselines.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-5.08-1 (folio_dispute_state context status).** `context.status` is the item's latest dispute status value, or null when the item was never disputed.
- **Prohibition canon-referral.** "The staff route must not act on an item outside the URL's folio" is an access-control (OWASP IDOR) rule, so it is not minted as a prohibition; T-05-05 and the scoped-binding 404 truth enforce it.
- **FA-5.08-2 (staff raise reuses the guest action).** Staff raise goes through `RaiseFolioDisputeAction` with a `User`, so the one-open rule and the lock are shared; staff may raise on open and settled folios alike.

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-6.02-1 (listener failures are reported, not thrown).** Both listeners are synchronous and run after the check-out or request has committed. A thrown exception would turn a committed check-out into a 500 and invite a retry that then fails with `reservation_state`. They `report()` and continue instead; `housekeeping:reconcile` lists the dirty room and `POST /housekeeping/tasks` (plan 06-04) recovers it, as D-08 anticipates.
- **FA-6.02-2 (arrival priority).** "Arrival waiting" is a `confirmed` reservation with `check_in` = hotel-local today holding the room through a reservation line; checked-in stays and pending holds do not count.

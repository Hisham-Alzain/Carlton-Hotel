<objective>
Give housekeeping tasks their single writers and wire them to rooms and requests (HK-03, HK-04 closing half; D-03, D-04, D-05, D-07, D-11, D-24 lang keys): the status writer with the transition table and the turnover room hook, the assign writer, the room-board closer, and the two-way link between a request task and its service request. After this plan a housekeeper who marks a room clean on the existing room board also closes its turnover task, and closing a service request from the existing operations queue cancels its task.

Output: three housekeeping actions, two exceptions, all housekeeping lang keys, the room-board closer hook, the service-request arm cancelling tasks, and their tests.
</objective>

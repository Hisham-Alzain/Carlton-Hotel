<objective>
List departure services (SVC-02) and seed the two catalogue categories that feed them and the guest quick-request chips (SVC-04 data; D-18, D-19, D-21, D-23): `late_checkout` (reception) and `luggage` (concierge) direct categories with unpriced default items, and `GET /departure-services`, an unpaginated projection of transfers, late checkouts, luggage requests and express checkouts for stays departing on a hotel-local date. After this plan a guest can ask for a late checkout from the app and the front desk sees it on the departures screen.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4, first sentence ("GET /departure-services lists transfers, late checkout, luggage ... and express checkout ... with stage, allowed_statuses and meta.truncated") and the data half of criterion 3 (chips map to direct-category items).
Output: two seeded categories, the Department routing, booking transition methods, a transfer factory state, `DepartureServiceProjection`, the departures service, request, controller and route, and the tests.
</objective>

## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-6.07-1 (half-open day).** D-18 says "UTC BETWEEN"; the window is start-inclusive, end-exclusive so a check-out at exactly hotel midnight belongs to the next day only, never both.
- **FA-6.07-2 (express row status).** D-21 fixes stage `resolved` and read-only for express checkouts but not their `status`; rows use `completed`, which is also a valid status filter value.
- **FA-6.07-3 (scheduled_at per kind).** Requests have no requested time (`ServiceRequest.requested_for` is deferred), so their `scheduled_at` is null and they sort after timed rows; express rows use `checked_out_at`.
- **FA-6.07-4 (per-source cap).** Each source query fetches at most 501 rows before the merge, so `truncated` is exact for a single source and conservative across sources.
- **FA-6.07-5 (SVC-04 concurrency, unresolved).** The edge probe asks what happens when a chip is tapped twice at once; the existing `POST /service-requests` has no dedupe, so two requests are created. Not changed in this phase (guest route contract); flagged for the mobile team.

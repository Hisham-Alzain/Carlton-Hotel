## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-6.08-1 (resolution scope).** The PATCH only resolves sources that can be departure rows (transfer bookings, late_checkout / luggage requests, guest-express reservations); anything else is 404 even if the uuid exists, so the route cannot be used to change spa or dining bookings. It does not re-check that the row belongs to a date inside today ± 30.
- **FA-6.08-2 (request transitions).** Requests keep the existing contract: any request status is accepted (no transition table), exactly as on the operations queue.
- **FA-6.08-3 (booking audit).** Bookings have no status-history table; the explicit activity entry carries from, to and reason, beside the LogsActivity diff.

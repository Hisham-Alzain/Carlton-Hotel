# 09.1-03 — DeleteGuestAccountAction (D-07, D-08, D-09, D-11)

## Files

- `backend/app/Actions/Guest/DeleteGuestAccountAction.php` (new).
- Tests: `tests/Feature/Auth/GuestAccountDeletionGuardTest.php` (18), `tests/Feature/Guests/DeleteGuestAccountActionTest.php` (13). RED first (class missing), then green.

## Behaviour

- One `DB::transaction`; `guests` row `lockForUpdate` first (asserted with `RecordsRowLocks`); an already-deleted guest returns `['data'=>null,'code'=>200]` without writing (D-09).
- Guards under the lock collect every reason in fixed order; booking codes sorted, at most 10 (live stays + open-folio stays). Throws `GuestAccountDeletionBlockedException` with `{reasons, booking_codes}`; nothing written (D-07).
- The scrub runs inside `activity()->withoutLogging()`: guest PII nulled and status/deleted_at set; all Sanctum tokens and device tokens deleted; notifications, staff notes and OTP rows for the old phone/email deleted; `reservations.phone` nulled; documents on stays that are not `checked_out` deleted (files after commit); conversations closed; guest-authored messages' body/attachment nulled (files after commit, Firestore `chats/{uuid}` re-mirrored with `body:null, attachment_path:null, redacted:true` after commit) (D-08).
- One explicit `guest.account_deleted` activity entry (subject = causer = guest, `properties.retained` counts only).
- Folios, folio items, payments, reviews, tickets, event inquiries, service bookings and service requests are byte-identical before and after (row hashes).

## Deviations

1. **activity log v5 stores attributes/old in `attribute_changes`**, not `properties` (the research assumed `properties`). Redaction rewrites both columns.
2. **Redaction is wider than the D-08 row** "subject = this guest": it also strips `phone` from the audit rows of the guest's reservations, and `body`/`attachment_path` from the audit rows of the redacted messages. Without that, D-22's "the old phone string appears in no activity_log row" cannot hold (Reservation and Message both log those columns). `last_name` on reservation rows stays, matching the retained `reservations.last_name`.
3. After-commit file deletion is wrapped in `try/catch (Throwable)` + `Log::warning` (R-4: the erasure already committed; a storage failure must not turn it into a 500). This is the only catch, and it's allowed by the plan's prohibition.
4. The Firestore payload mirrors `SendMessageAction`'s keys (`attachment_path`, not `attachment_url` as CONTEXT D-08 words it), plus `redacted: true`.

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 10 goal (verbatim): "Guests earn integer points once per settled folio, see a FIFO-expiring balance and ledger, redeem catalog rewards into vouchers and pay part of a booking with points; cancellations undo every loyalty effect without ever producing a negative balance. Staff configure the six program values, manage the catalog, adjust points with an audited reason and report issued/redeemed/expired points." This plan is "see a FIFO-expiring balance and ledger" for guests and staff.

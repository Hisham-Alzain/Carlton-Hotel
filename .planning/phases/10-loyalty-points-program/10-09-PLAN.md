## Flagged Assumptions

- **FA-10.09-1.** Voucher expiry is computed in hotel-local days (end of the local day `voucher_valid_days` from today), mirroring batch expiry, so a guest never loses a voucher mid-day.
- **FA-10.09-2.** True concurrent redeem with one key is MySQL-only (10-VALIDATION manual list); on SQLite the guest lock is asserted via `RecordsRowLocks` and the unique ledger key is the backstop.

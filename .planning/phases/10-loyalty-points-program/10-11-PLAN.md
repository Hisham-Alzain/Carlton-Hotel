<objective>
Let an authenticated guest pay part of a booking with points or one voucher on `POST /api/reservations`, atomically with reservation creation and idempotent under `Idempotency-Key`, and show the applied loyalty on the reservation (LOY-16; Q5, Q6, Q12, Q16, Q17, M-4, M-5, M-6, M-7).

Purpose: the booking flow the Flutter app already mocks; the discount reduces `total_usd`, so the folio and later earn automatically use the net amount.
Output: 1 new action, 4 modified booking files, 2 test files.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 10 goal (verbatim): "Guests earn integer points once per settled folio, see a FIFO-expiring balance and ledger, redeem catalog rewards into vouchers and pay part of a booking with points; cancellations undo every loyalty effect without ever producing a negative balance. Staff configure the six program values, manage the catalog, adjust points with an audited reason and report issued/redeemed/expired points." This plan is "pay part of a booking with points" (and with a voucher).

Roles: Task 1 = Opus QA (RED), Task 2 = Opus engineer (GREEN).
